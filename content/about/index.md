## Research Motivation

I am curious about how carbon-based life and silicon-based systems can understand and connect with one another. My early work with autonomous vehicles and diagnostic devices approached this question from two directions: helping machines sense and navigate the physical world, and using sensors to better understand human health. The release of ChatGPT in late 2022, followed by the emergence of LLMs in China, drew me toward language as a new interface between people and machines.

Starting with LLM applications, I moved into model fundamentals, post-training, and Agentic RL to explore how systems learn to reason and act through feedback. My work on RSI asks how they might learn to improve themselves. Full-duplex omnimodal models bring these interests together through continuous listening, seeing, and responding—toward more natural interaction with people.

## Looking Ahead

Once this work reaches meaningful milestones, I hope to explore machine olfaction and taste, integrating new senses into interaction models. My long-term vision is a “machine brain” that combines sensory information, understands its environment, and guides action. Alongside self-improvement, this could offer a path toward more comprehensive, all-sensory intelligence—and an exploration of what an early form of silicon-based life might look like.
